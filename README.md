# 📊 Customer Churn Analysis Using Business Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer churn using Business Analytics techniques. The objective is to understand customer behavior, identify factors affecting churn, and provide data-driven business recommendations. The project includes data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), statistical analysis, hypothesis testing, and a business implementation plan.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Customer churn is one of the biggest challenges for businesses because losing customers directly impacts revenue and profitability.

The company wants to answer the following questions:

- Why are customers leaving?
- Which customers are most likely to churn?
- Does contract type affect customer retention?
- Do monthly charges influence customer churn?
- Which payment methods are preferred?

---

# 🎯 Project Objectives

- Clean and prepare customer data.
- Perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).
- Identify customer churn patterns.
- Apply descriptive statistics.
- Perform correlation analysis.
- Conduct hypothesis testing.
- Generate business insights.
- Provide business recommendations.
- Create an implementation plan.

# 💡 Key Business Insights

- Customers with Month-to-Month contracts show higher churn.
- Long-term customers are more loyal.
- Higher Monthly Charges are associated with customer churn.
- Paperless Billing is widely adopted.
- Digital payment methods are commonly used.
- Longer customer tenure contributes to higher customer lifetime value.

---

# ✅ Business Recommendations

- Promote One-Year and Two-Year contracts.
- Introduce customer loyalty programs.
- Offer personalized retention discounts.
- Improve customer support for high-risk customers.
- Encourage digital payment methods.
- Monitor churn trends regularly.
